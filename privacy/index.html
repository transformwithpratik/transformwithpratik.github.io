Privacy notice

Last updated 1 November 2026

GetFitWithPratik is run by Pratik Jain. This notice explains what personal data the website and client portal collect, why, and what control you have over it. Questions: transformwithpratik@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Enquiries: the details you enter in the coaching form, such as your name, email, WhatsApp number, age, height, weight, goals, schedule, food preferences and any health information you choose to share.
  • Feedback: your rating and comments, and your name and email only if you provide them.
  • Client accounts: your login email, profile details, coaching plans, food and workout logs, measurements, weekly check-ins, progress photos, messages from your coach and payment records.
  • Technical data: your browser stores your login session and a few preferences (such as units) on your device. The site does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

Why

  • To reply to your enquiry and set up a consult call.
  • To deliver coaching: write your plan, review your progress and send you feedback and reminders.
  • To publish a review or transformation, only when you have explicitly agreed. You can withdraw that permission at any time.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored with Supabase (database, login and file storage), hosted in Singapore. Emails are sent through a transactional email provider. Only Pratik and any coach he authorises can see client data. Progress photos are stored privately and are shown only through short-lived links to you and your coach. Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not become clients are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept while you are a client and for up to 24 months afterwards so you can return, unless you ask for them to be deleted sooner. Payment records may be kept longer where the law requires.

Your choices

  • See and export your data: clients can download everything from Profile, then Export my data.
  • Correct it: edit your profile in the portal or email me.
  • Delete it: clients can delete their account from the profile page; this removes your logs, check-ins and photos. Anyone can email me to delete an enquiry or feedback.
  • Stop emails: turn off email notifications in your profile.

These choices reflect your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, contact me first and I will put it right.

Health information

Coaching is not medical care. Health details you share are used only to plan safe training and nutrition, and I may ask you to get clearance from a doctor before starting.
