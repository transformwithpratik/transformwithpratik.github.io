# WS4 — YouTube launch

## Goal
A YouTube channel that builds deeper trust than reels can (longer explainers,
case studies) and sends viewers to the website and coaching enquiry.

## Decisions to make
1. Channel name/handle — consistent with the Instagram handle decision and the
   website brand (see WS3 brand mismatch).
2. Positioning line, e.g. "Science-backed fitness for busy professionals".
3. Format mix: long-form (8–15 min explainers, myth-busts, "SWE day of eating",
   client case studies with permission) + Shorts repurposed from WS2 reels.
4. Cadence that's sustainable alongside a full-time job (e.g. 1 long-form every
   2 weeks + 3 Shorts/week).
5. Thumbnail and title style.

## Launch process
1. Name + handle + channel art + about section + links (website, IG).
2. Trailer (60–90s) and 3 launch videos ready before announcing.
3. Video SEO: title keyword, description with chapters and sources, tags,
   pinned comment with CTA.
4. Cross-promotion: IG story/reel teasers, website embeds (WS3).
5. Review analytics after 8 videos (CTR, retention, enquiries) and adjust.

## Open questions
- Hindi, English or Hinglish for long-form?
- Talking-head setup: mic, camera, location?
- Same handle across IG and YouTube?
