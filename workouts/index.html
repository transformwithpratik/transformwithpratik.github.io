Two weekly templates that fit a full-time job

Training each muscle about twice a week with a few hard sets works well for most people. Pick the one that matches the days you can reliably train.

3 days: full body

Monday, Wednesday, Friday. About 45 minutes.

  • Squat pattern: goblet squat or back squat, 3 sets of 6 to 10
  • Push: push-up or bench press, 3 sets of 6 to 12
  • Pull: row or lat pulldown, 3 sets of 8 to 12
  • Hinge: Romanian deadlift or hip thrust, 3 sets of 8 to 12
  • Core or carry: plank, dead bug or farmer carry, 2 sets

4 days: upper and lower

Upper, Lower, rest, Upper, Lower. About 40 to 50 minutes.

  • Upper: one press, one row, one vertical pull, one shoulder and one arm exercise
  • Lower: one squat, one hinge, one single-leg exercise, calves and core
  • 2 to 4 sets each, stopping 1 to 3 reps before failure
  • Add a little weight or a rep when every set hits the top of the range

On top of training, aim for regular walking. The WHO recommends 150 to 300 minutes of moderate activity a week plus muscle-strengthening on two or more days. If you have pain, an injury or a heart condition, get advice from a doctor or physiotherapist before starting.
