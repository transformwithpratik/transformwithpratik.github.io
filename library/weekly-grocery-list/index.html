Weekly grocery list

2 min read · By Pratik Jain

A vegetarian high-protein kitchen for one person for a week. Tick what you already have; your ticks are saved on this device. Use Save as PDF to take it to the shop.

Protein

Dals and pulses

Grains

Vegetables

Fruit

Nuts, seeds and snacks

Flavour and cooking

Sunday prep, 45 minutes

  1. Soak and pressure cook a big batch of chana or rajma. It keeps 4 days in the fridge.
  2. Tie 500 g curd in a muslin cloth to hang overnight.
  3. Soak whole moong to sprout by Tuesday.
  4. Wash and chop salad vegetables into a box.
  5. Portion nuts into small boxes for the week.

General information, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or take medication, check with your doctor before changing your diet, training or supplements.
