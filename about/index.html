    How I coach

    What you can expect

    • Advice grounded in research, with a clear line between solid evidence and early findings.
    • A plan built around your job, travel and family, not an ideal week you will never have.
    • Vegetarian-first nutrition with Indian food you already eat.
    • Weekly feedback based on your data: weight trend, measurements, training and how you feel.
    • Honest answers, including "this is not a fit for coaching right now".

    What I will not do

    • Crash diets, detoxes or cutting out whole food groups.
    • Guaranteed results, before-and-after promises or fake countdown offers.
    • Shame anyone about their body, food or past attempts.
    • Replace your doctor. For medical conditions, I work alongside their advice.
