Built for people whose week is already full.

Most programmes assume you have unlimited time and a cook at home. This one starts from your real constraints.

  • You have started over more times than you can count.Monday plans that collapse by Thursday are a planning problem, not a willpower problem. We build a routine that survives deadlines, late calls and travel.
  • You sit for nine hours a day.Short strength sessions, more daily steps and a few desk habits do more for you than an occasional heroic workout.
  • You are vegetarian and protein feels impossible.Meal plans are built from dal, paneer, curd, soy, sprouts and the food you already eat, with a clear daily protein target.
  • You cannot tell what is actually working.Weekly check-ins track your weight trend, measurements, training and sleep, so every change to your plan is based on your own data.
The method

Structured, measurable, and adjusted every week.

No crash diets and no banned foods. A plan built on the basics that hold up in research, tracked simply, and changed when your data says so.

Start your transformation

  1. Consult call

    A short call about your goal, schedule, food preferences, injuries and what has got in the way before.

  2. Your plan

    Calorie and protein targets, a training plan for your equipment and time, and a meal plan with Indian vegetarian options.

  3. Track in the portal

    Log food, workouts, steps, water and sleep from your phone. Most days it takes two or three minutes.

  4. Weekly check-in

    Send your weight, measurements and how the week went. Pratik reviews it and replies with feedback.

  5. Adjust and progress

    Targets and training change based on your results, so progress keeps coming after the first few weeks.
