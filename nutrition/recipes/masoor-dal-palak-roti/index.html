Masoor dal palak with two rotis

Everyday dal made heartier with spinach, served with two rotis and a spoon of ghee. A complete plate.

458kcal
22 gprotein
81 gcarbs
8 gfat

Per serving. Serves 1. Prep 10 min, cook 20 min.

Ingredients

  • 50 g masoor dal
  • A large handful of spinach
  • ½ tomato
  • ¼ onion
  • 60 g atta, for 2 rotis
  • 1 tsp ghee, for tadka
  • Jeera, garlic, red chilli, turmeric, salt

Method

  1. Pressure cook the dal with turmeric, salt and 1½ cups water for 2 whistles.
  2. Add chopped spinach and tomato and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. For the tadka, heat ghee, add jeera, garlic, onion and red chilli, and pour over the dal.
  4. Make two thin rotis from the atta and serve.
Tip. A katori of curd (150 g) on the side adds about 5 g of protein.

Nutrition is estimated from standard food-composition values (USDA FoodData Central, IFCT 2017 and typical pack labels), with spices counted as zero. Brands and portion sizes vary, so treat the numbers as close estimates.
