Banana peanut butter whey shake

A post-workout shake with protein, carbs to refuel and a little peanut butter for taste.

413kcal
35 gprotein
39 gcarbs
15 gfat

Per serving. Serves 1. Prep 3 min.

Ingredients

  • 250 ml toned milk
  • 1 scoop (30 g) whey protein
  • 1 medium banana
  • 2 tsp peanut butter
  • Ice, cinnamon

Method

  1. Blend everything for 30 seconds and drink.
Tip. For fat loss, use water instead of half the milk and skip the peanut butter: about 130 kcal less.
