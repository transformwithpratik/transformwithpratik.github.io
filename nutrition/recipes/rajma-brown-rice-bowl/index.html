Rajma brown rice bowl

Classic rajma with brown rice and a crunchy cucumber salad. Plenty of fibre to keep you full through the afternoon.

439kcal
17 gprotein
79 gcarbs
7 gfat

Per serving. Serves 1. Prep 10 min, cook 40 min.

Ingredients

  • 50 g rajma, soaked overnight
  • 50 g brown rice
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 large tomato, pureed
  • 1 tsp oil
  • ½ cucumber, diced, for salad
  • Ginger-garlic paste, rajma masala or garam masala, salt

Method

  1. Pressure cook the soaked rajma with salt for 5 to 6 whistles until fully soft.
  2. Cook the brown rice with twice its volume of water for about 25 minutes, or 3 whistles in a cooker.
  3. Heat the oil, cook the onion until brown, add ginger-garlic paste, then the tomato and masala. Cook until oil separates.
  4. Add the rajma with its water and simmer 10 minutes, mashing a few beans to thicken.
  5. Serve over the rice with the cucumber salad on the side.
Tip. Cook rajma in bulk on Sunday. It keeps 4 days in the fridge and freezes well.
