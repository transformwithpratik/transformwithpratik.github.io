Overnight oats with whey and chia

Mix it in two minutes at night, eat it cold the next morning. About 37 g of protein for a commute breakfast.

499kcal
37 gprotein
61 gcarbs
13 gfat

Per serving. Serves 1. Prep 5 min.

Ingredients

  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 150 ml toned milk
  • 1 scoop (30 g) whey protein
  • 2 tsp chia seeds
  • ½ banana, sliced

Method

  1. In a jar, whisk the whey into the milk first so it does not clump.
  2. Stir in the oats and chia seeds. Close the jar and refrigerate overnight.
  3. In the morning, top with banana. Loosen with a splash of milk if it is too thick.
Tip. No whey? Use 150 g hung curd instead of the whey and half the milk.
