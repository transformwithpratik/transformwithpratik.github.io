Chana chaat

Tangy chickpea salad you can assemble at your desk. No cooking if you keep boiled chana in the fridge.

275kcal
14 gprotein
48 gcarbs
4 gfat

Per serving. Serves 1. Prep 10 min.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup boiled kabuli chana (150 g)
  • ¼ onion, chopped
  • ½ tomato, chopped
  • ¼ cucumber, chopped
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Chaat masala, roasted jeera, green chilli, coriander, salt

Method

  1. Combine the chana, onion, tomato and cucumber in a bowl.
  2. Add lemon juice, chaat masala, roasted jeera, chilli and salt. Toss.
  3. Top with coriander and eat within a few hours so the vegetables stay crisp.
Tip. Add 50 g crumbled paneer for about 9 g more protein.

Nutrition is estimated from standard food-composition values (USDA FoodData Central, IFCT 2017 and typical pack labels), with spices counted as zero. Brands and portion sizes vary, so treat the numbers as close estimates.
