Soya chunk pulao with raita

A one-pot lunch where soya chunks do the heavy lifting on protein. Packs well for the office.

528kcal
31 gprotein
75 gcarbs
11 gfat

Per serving. Serves 1. Prep 15 min, cook 20 min.

Ingredients

  • 40 g soya chunks (dry weight)
  • 60 g rice, rinsed and soaked 15 minutes
  • ¼ cup green peas
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1½ tsp oil or ghee
  • 100 g curd, for raita
  • Bay leaf, jeera, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, salt

Method

  1. Soak the soya chunks in hot salted water for 10 minutes, then squeeze out all the water.
  2. Heat the oil in a pressure cooker, add jeera and bay leaf, then the onion. Cook until golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste, the soya chunks, peas and garam masala. Stir for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the drained rice and 1¼ cups water with salt. Pressure cook for 2 whistles and rest 10 minutes.
  5. Whisk the curd with salt and roasted jeera and serve on the side.
Tip. Squeezing the soaked chunks well is what makes them soak up the masala instead of tasting rubbery.
